About GSP

The General Science Program (GSP) is an innovative educational initiative designed to empower elementary students by providing them with the tools and inspiration to explore their surrounding environment.

Through engaging activities and STEAM-based hands-on projects, GSP builds motivation for learning and expands knowledge about career exploration.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯

Mission

To inspire and empower elementary students through interactive STEAM education, fostering curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong passion for learning and discovery.

Vision

For Elementary Students

Who Can Join?

GSP is designed specifically for elementary students (grades K-5) who are curious about the world around them and eager to learn through hands-on exploration.

  • ✓ Students who love asking "Why?" and "How?"
  • ✓ Young learners interested in science and technology
  • ✓ Kids who enjoy building and creating things
  • ✓ Those curious about different career possibilities
  • ✓ All students, regardless of prior experience

Frequently Asked Questions

What age group is GSP designed for?

+

GSP is designed for elementary students in grades K-5 (ages 5-11). We tailor our activities and projects to be age-appropriate for different grade levels.

Do students need prior experience in STEAM subjects?

+

No prior experience is necessary! GSP is designed to welcome students at all skill levels. Our instructors provide guidance and support to ensure every student can participate and learn.

What materials do students need to bring?

+

All materials and supplies are provided by GSP. Students just need to bring their curiosity, creativity, and enthusiasm to learn!

How much does the program cost?

+

Program costs vary depending on the format chosen. We offer scholarships and financial aid for families in need. Contact us for detailed pricing information.

Where are GSP sessions held?

+

We partner with local schools and community centers. Session locations are provided upon registration based on your area.

What is the student-to-instructor ratio?

+

We maintain a low ratio of 10:1 to ensure each student receives personalized attention and support during activities and projects.
